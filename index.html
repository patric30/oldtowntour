Check the clock before you start

The Glockenspiel plays at 11:00 and 12:00 daily, plus 17:00 from March to October, and runs 12–15 minutes. Either start 20 minutes before one of those and let it open the tour, or start after it finishes. Getting caught mid-tour costs you the whole buffer.

How the timing works

Google walks the loop in 32 minutes. A group of eight that stops to look does not — the walk budgets below are Google's times plus 20%, which is where the 39 minutes comes from. Tap Start tour and the bar at the top tracks you against the plan. Green means you have room; red means take a cut from the panel at the bottom.

Running late

Take these in order. Each one is written to be invisible to the group.

  1. Don't stop at Café Luitpold. Point at it, tell the Prince Regent line while walking past. saves 2 min
  2. Skip going inside the Frauenkirche. Tell the Devil's Footstep story from the square instead — the door is right there and the story survives it. saves 3 min
  3. Cut the Residenz lions and the Wagner list at Max-Joseph-Platz down to one sentence each. saves 2 min
  4. From Café Luitpold, go straight down Theatinerstraße instead of the Salvatorplatz route. Less pretty, more direct. saves 2 min

When you finish

You are back at Marienplatz with a group that has been standing for an hour. Three options within four minutes' walk:

  • Viktualienmarkt — 2 min south. Open-air market, beer garden under the maypole, best if the weather holds.
  • Schneider Bräuhaus — 3 min east on Tal. Weissbier and the least touristy of the big halls.
  • Café Luitpold — 9 min back north. Worth it if the group wants to sit somewhere quiet and grand.
